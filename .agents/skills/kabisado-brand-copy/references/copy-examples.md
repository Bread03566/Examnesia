# Kabisado Copy Reference Bank

Use these patterns as anchors for voice consistency across views.

## Quiz Score Tiers
| Score Range | Example Banner |
|---|---|
| 90–100% | `"Hala siya, Dean's Lister yarn?! 🦉✨"` |
| 70–89% | `"Solid! Konting polish na lang sa naligaw na items."` |
| 50–69% | `"Nasa gitna pa — isa pang round sa weak topics, kaya na 'to!"` |
| Below 50% | `"Warm-up pa lang 'yan. Balikan natin ang explanations at flashcards mo."` |

## Toast Notifications (Short & Action-Confirmed)
- Upload success: `"Pasok na ang module! Handa na ang topics mo."`
- Flashcard saved: `"Na-save na ang flashcard."`
- Flag submitted: `"Salamat sa report! Na-log na ang feedback mo."`
- Offline engine notice: `"Offline study engine active — grounded sa notes mo lang."`

## Serious / Zero-Joke Surfaces (Always Clear)
- **Login throttle:** `"Too many attempts. Please wait a few minutes before trying again."`
- **Account deletion:** `"This permanently deletes your account, uploaded modules, flashcards, and quiz history. This cannot be undone."`
- **AI consent:** State clearly what text is sent, that Gemini is optional, and that Kabisado works offline without it.
