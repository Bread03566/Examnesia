Privacy: what we store

Short and plain. No ads, no tracking, and we do not sell your data.

What is stored

  • Your Gmail address and display name.
  • A password hash (Argon2id) and short-lived hashed password-reset codes when you request a reset — never your password in plain text. We cannot read it.
  • Session information: when you signed in, whether the session is on the web or the Android app, and which browser/device (hashed), so you can be signed out of other devices.
  • Your study content: the extracted text of the modules you upload, summaries, flashcards, quizzes, scores, and progress. The original PDF or Word (.docx) file is not kept; it is discarded immediately once its text is extracted.

Your Gmail address is used only to sign in and send 6-digit password reset codes — never for marketing or ads.

Android app & device permissions

  • The Examnesia Android app requests only internet and network status permissions. It does not request access to your contacts, camera, location, or background files.
  • On Android, your sign-in refresh token is encrypted using the device’s hardware-backed Android Keystore and excluded from cloud auto-backup.

Why

So you can sign in, keep your study materials separate from everyone else’s, and move between the web app and the Android app without losing progress.

How long

Sign-in and session records are deleted after 30 days. Your study content stays until you delete your account.

Who runs the app (processors)

  • Render hosts the backend API and web application.
  • Supabase stores the PostgreSQL database.
  • GitHub hosts the source repository, this public landing page (GitHub Pages), and signed Android APK releases (GitHub Releases).

AI (Gemini)

Gemini is switched off by default. None of your module content is sent to any external AI service. The app uses its built-in offline study engine for summaries, flashcards, quizzes, and chat.

Even if the administrator enables Gemini availability on the server, it remains strictly opt-in: only students aged 18 or older who independently confirm their age and grant consent in the account menu have their study prompts sent to Google Gemini. You can withdraw consent at any time in the account menu, and your choice is recorded in a tamper-evident log.

Deleting your account

Open your account menu in the web or Android app and enter your password to delete your account. Your account and all study content attached to it are removed immediately. You can export a copy of everything first with Download my data, or deactivate your account to prevent sign-ins without deleting data. Account deletion cannot be undone.
