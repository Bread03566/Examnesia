Android Installation Instructions

Because Examnesia is distributed directly as a signed APK from GitHub Releases (outside the Google Play Store), Android asks for your confirmation on the first install.

1

Download the signed APK

Tap Download APK above on your Android phone (running Android 7.0 or newer). Verify that the version matches the release card.

2

Allow “Install unknown apps” once

When you open the downloaded .apk file, Android may prompt you to allow your browser (e.g., Chrome) to install unknown apps. Toggle it on for this install; you can turn it back off afterward.

3

Play Protect & developer verification prompts

Because the APK is sideloaded directly rather than downloaded from Google Play, Google Play Protect may display an unknown-developer scan prompt. Only install APKs downloaded directly from this official site and GitHub Releases matching the listed SHA-256 checksum.

4

In-app update notifications

Once installed, the Examnesia Android app checks the signed release manifest automatically, notifies you when a newer version is available, verifies the new APK’s SHA-256 checksum and signing certificate, and hands installation to Android’s system package installer.

Frequently Asked Questions

Honest answers about accounts, cross-platform sync, privacy, and hosting.

Do I need separate accounts for the Web App and the Android App?

No. Both platforms connect to the exact same backend and database. Sign in with the same Gmail address and password on your computer browser and your Android phone — your uploaded modules, summaries, flashcards, quiz scores, and study streak are shared automatically.

How do I reset a forgotten password?

Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in screen in either the Web App or the Android App. Enter your registered @gmail.com address to receive a 6-digit reset code valid for 15 minutes.

Is my uploaded study material sent to external AI?

Not by default. Examnesia’s external Gemini AI integration is switched off by default, and summaries, flashcards, quizzes, and chat run on Examnesia’s built-in offline study engine. Even if the server administrator enables Gemini availability, your content is never sent unless you confirm you are 18+ and explicitly opt in inside your account settings.

Why does the Web App sometimes take up to a minute to open?

To keep Examnesia 100% free (₱0/month), the backend server runs on a free Render instance that sleeps after 15 minutes of inactivity. Clicking Open Web App on this page automatically wakes the server and takes you straight into the app as soon as it responds.

Does the Android app work offline?

In version 1, the Android app detects when your internet connection drops, displays a clear offline notice, and keeps your unsent form input on screen until you reconnect. Signing in, loading modules, and saving quiz attempts require an internet connection to the shared server.
